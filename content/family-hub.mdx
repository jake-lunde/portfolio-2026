<Section no="03" label="The system" title="A screensaver with a job. A dashboard with a gate.">
  Two modes, really. **Ambient** is the screensaver with a job. It tells the
  house what&rsquo;s next and who&rsquo;s where without anyone asking.
  **Active** is when you walk up and use it. Full dashboard, modal views,
  and you can filter the whole thing down to one family member.

  Then there&rsquo;s the gate. Privacy was the top-ranked function in our
  research, so adults manage, kids view, and a PIN sits between glancing at
  the thing and actually doing something on it. Approving chores, moving
  money, editing the calendar.

  <Plate cap="Plate 03 · the interaction model" fig="FIG. C" caption="Switch modes. This is the spec the whole product hangs off (ix-models canvas: 453-342132).">

<Section no="06" label="Ten to one" title="One designer, ten engineers.">
  <Lead>
    Functionally, the Family Hub design team was me. One designer, roughly

  states and polish behind them. That was scary, but it worked because the
